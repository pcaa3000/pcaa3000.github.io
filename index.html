About / Philosophy

Infrastructure, automation and AI working as one system.

I am a Platform Engineer and Developer with a systems-first mindset. For years I ran the platform stack at Clínica Ricardo Palma (NTC/Canvia): service-desk tooling, monitoring and observability, database administration, and cross-platform automation.

Most outages and delays are not technology problems — they are friction problems. My work removes that friction with containers, automation and AI: faster proof-of-concepts, fewer manual tasks, and clearer visibility into what is actually running.

Faster PoCs

By containerizing GLPI, Metabase and internal tooling, I cut the time to stand up a working proof-of-concept from days to hours.

Full Visibility

Built monitoring and observability on Zabbix, Grafana and Loki so the team sees problems before users report them.

Automated Toil

Replaced repetitive operating tasks — report generation, AD users, AWS instance management — with Python, PowerShell and Bash.
